Why this community exists

Organised enough that everyone knows what is happening, open enough that anyone can walk in.

Plenty of people here are already building something on their own. What was missing was an obvious place to find each other.

It is run by students, for students, and open to developers outside the university too. You will always know who to ask.

We would rather be small and useful than large and noisy.

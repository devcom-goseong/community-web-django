Why this community exists

Organised enough that everyone knows what is happening, open enough that anyone can walk in.

Plenty of students at KDU are teaching themselves to code, sketching product ideas, or quietly working on something interesting on their own. What was missing was an obvious place to find each other. This is that place.

The community is run by students, for students, and it is open to developers outside the university too. A leadership team keeps the rules, the platforms and the activities in place so that anyone who joins knows what to expect.

We would rather be small and useful than large and noisy. Activities start at a size we can genuinely run, and grow as more people step forward to take responsibility for them.
